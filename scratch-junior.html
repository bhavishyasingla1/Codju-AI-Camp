Module 1: Meet the Blocks & First Animation

Tour the canvas, learn green flag triggers, forward and backward motion, and create a dancing cat sprite.

Module 2: Custom Characters & Voice Recording

Customize sprites with the paint editor, add multi-page backdrops, and record personal voices to speak dialogue.
