ROADMAP TO MASTERY

5-Module Curriculum

From Blocks & Logic to Publishing & Sharing.

Module 1: Blocks & Logic Foundations

Sessions 1 & 2

Get acquainted with the Scratch 3.0 interface. Master coordinate systems (X: -240 to 240, Y: -180 to 180), glide mechanics, arrow key controls, and event triggers.

  • ✓Understanding the 2D Cartesian Coordinate Grid
  • ✓Keypress event listeners & smooth movement
  • ✓Costume animations & sprite flipping
  • ✓Forever loops vs Repeat Until blocks

Module 2: Variables & Scoring Systems

Sessions 3 & 4

Learn how computer programs store information in memory. Create variables for Score, Player Lives, Level Timer, and high score tracking with cloud variables.

  • ✓Creating, modifying & resetting variables
  • ✓Countdown timers using the built-in clock
  • ✓Random number generation (pick random 1 to 10)
  • ✓Conditionals: If score > 100 then You Win

Module 3: Game Physics & Collisions

Sessions 5 & 6

Transform static sprites into dynamic arcade games! Program gravity simulation, jump velocities, edge bouncing, obstacle cloning, and hitbox sensing.

  • ✓Gravity formula: Change Y by Velocity
  • ✓Sensing blocks: touching color & touching sprite
  • ✓Sprite cloning for laser blasts & falling coins
  • ✓Sound effects & audio pitch modulation

Module 4: Broadcasts & Multi-Level Worlds

Sessions 7 & 8

Learn inter-sprite communication using Broadcast Messages. Build multi-stage level transitions, Start Menu screens, Game Over screens, and a Boss Battle encounter.

  • ✓Broadcast & Receive message triggers
  • ✓Designing Start Menus & Game Over interfaces
  • ✓Boss battle AI logic & attack patterns
  • ✓Multi-backdrop level progression

Module 5: Publish & Share to MIT Community

Sessions 9 & 10

Professional game polish! Add instructions, notes, credits, custom animated game thumbnails, and publish to the Codju Scratch Studio. Final demo day presentation.

  • ✓Code refactoring & comment documentation
  • ✓Writing clear player instructions & credits
  • ✓Publishing to MIT Scratch public studio
  • ✓Graduation showcase & verified digital certificate
