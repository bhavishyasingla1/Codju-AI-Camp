STEP-BY-STEP SYLLABUS

5-Module Curriculum

From meeting the first blocks to publishing and celebrating their final animated storybook.

Module 1: Meet the Blocks & First Animation

Sessions 1 & 2

Introduce young minds to the playful world of ScratchJr. Learn the interface tour, understand green flag triggers, forward and backward motion, and create a dancing cat sprite.

  • ✓Interface tour: Canvas, palette & character tray
  • ✓Start on Green Flag block triggers
  • ✓Motion blocks: Right, left, up, down & hop
  • ✓Resetting sprite starting positions

Module 2: Custom Characters & Voice Recording

Sessions 3 & 4

Bring stories to life! Children customize sprites using the built-in paint editor, choose whimsical outdoor backgrounds, and record their own voices to speak dialogues.

  • ✓Using the Paint Editor to recolor characters
  • ✓Importing multiple stage backgrounds
  • ✓Microphone audio recording blocks
  • ✓Triggering actions when a character is tapped

Module 3: Speed, Loops & Scene Transitions

Sessions 5 & 6

Learn how repeat loops save block space. Program characters moving at slow, medium, and supersonic speeds, and seamlessly flip from one scene to the next.

  • ✓Orange control blocks & Repeat loops
  • ✓Speed modifiers: Walk vs Sprint vs Glide
  • ✓Growing & shrinking character sizing blocks
  • ✓Multi-page scene flipping triggers

Module 4: Collisions & Interactive Mini-Games

Session 7

Introduce game mechanics. What happens when two characters bump into each other? Learn collision triggers and secret color-coded mail messaging.

  • ✓Start on Bump (character collision trigger)
  • ✓Sending and opening color-coded letter blocks
  • ✓Disappearing & re-appearing magic blocks
  • ✓Building a two-player animal race

Module 5: Share It, Showcase & Certificate

Session 8

The grand graduation! Students record a live video walkthrough of their completed storybook, present it to fellow classmates, and receive their Codju certificate.

  • ✓Final project polish & bug fixes
  • ✓Screen recording & parent presentation
  • ✓Publishing to personal Codju student profile
  • ✓Felicitation ceremony & graduation badge
