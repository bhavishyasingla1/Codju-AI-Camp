## Overview
Codju Academy is an outcome-driven coding and artificial intelligence education platform designed for school students from Grades 1 to 12. Partnered with 250+ premier institutions across India, Codju delivers NEP 2020-aligned experiential tracks where students build real-world software, games, animations, and generative AI artifacts.

Every graduate receives a verified digital certificate with QR code verification and a permanent public portfolio profile (`codju.com/student/name`).
