STRUCTURED 10-SESSION SYLLABUS

5-Week Curriculum & Tool Stack

2 live sessions per week with step-by-step tool walkthroughs and instant recordings.

Week 01

Generative AI Foundations & Advanced Prompting

Tools Mastered
ChatGPT 4o · Claude 3.5 · Gemini 1.5 Pro
Session 1
Demystifying Large Language Models

How neural networks process tokens. Overcoming hallucinations, setting custom instructions, and the R-T-C-O (Role, Task, Context, Output) formula.

Session 2
Chain-of-Thought & Multi-Step Reasoning

Prompt chaining techniques, complex mathematical reasoning, debate simulations, and structured JSON output generation.

Week 02

Visual Creation & AI Art Direction

Tools Mastered
Midjourney · Ideogram · Canva Magic Studio
Session 3
Photorealism, Lighting & Camera Parameters

Aspect ratios (--ar 16:9), lighting styles (cinematic, volumetric, studio bokeh), and camera focal lengths.

Session 4
Consistent Characters & Typography Rendering

Using Ideogram for clear typography in logos and posters. Maintaining facial consistency across multiple scenes for graphic novels.

Week 03

AI Audio, Voice Synthesis & Video

Tools Mastered
Suno AI · ElevenLabs · Pika Labs · CapCut
Session 5
Music Composition & Voice Cloning

Composing vocal harmonies and genre blending in Suno. Cloning voices ethically in ElevenLabs to narrate documentary podcasts.

Session 6
Text-to-Video Animation & Sound Design

Directing cinematic motion prompts in Pika. Lip-sync synchronization, sound effects mixing, and final video editing in CapCut.

Week 04

Supercharged Academics & Research

Tools Mastered
NotebookLM · Perplexity Pro · Gamma.app
Session 7
Grounded Document Intelligence

Querying PDF textbooks with zero hallucinations in Google NotebookLM. Generating audio podcast discussions between two AI hosts.

Session 8
Deep Research & Executive Presentations

Citations synthesis in Perplexity. Automated slide generation and visual formatting for school presentations using Gamma.

Week 05

No-Code Web Apps & National Hackathon

Tools Mastered
Lovable.dev · Make.com · Custom GPTs
Session 9
Vibe Coding: English to Working Web Apps

Prompting full web applications with UI buttons, score trackers, and databases in Lovable. Connecting automation triggers with Make.com.

Session 10
Grand Capstone Hackathon & Graduation

Live project presentations, judge evaluations, certificate felicitation ceremony, and publishing to permanent public profiles.
